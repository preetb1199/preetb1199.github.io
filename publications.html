Findings of ACL · 2026

Prompt Injection in Automated Résumé Screening with Large Language Models: Single and Multi-Injection Settings

Preet Baxi, Jiannan Xu, Jane Yi Jiang, Stefanus Jasin · Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: ACL 2026, pp. 2942–2953 (July 2026) · First author · Peer-reviewed

Controlled study of subtle self-promotional prompt injections embedded in résumés during LLM-based candidate screening. Shows that injection reliably boosts rankings in homogeneous pools when rare, collapses as manipulation spreads, and can induce rank inversions that displace stronger applicants in heterogeneous pools.

Classical and Quantum Gravity · 2026

Monitoring of Continuous-Wave Hardware Injections in LIGO Interferometers during the O4 Observing Run

Preet Baxi, Jessica Leviton, Eilam Morag, Matthew Pitkin, Keith Riles · Classical and Quantum Gravity, Vol. 43, No. 13, 135019 (July 7, 2026) · First author · Peer-reviewed

Presents daily and weekly monitoring of 18 simulated continuous-wave (CW) hardware injections across Advanced LIGO during O4, comparing a templated matched-filter estimator, a frequentist F-statistic, and Bayesian reconstruction to verify detector amplitude, absolute phase over up to 10¹¹ cycles, and timing delays.

Physical Review D · 2021

Detectability of Gravitational Higher-Order Modes in the Third-Generation Era

Divyajyoti, Preet Baxi, Chandra Kant Mishra, K. G. Arun · Physical Review D, Vol. 104, No. 8, 084080 (October 21, 2021) · Peer-reviewed

Evaluates the detectability of subdominant spherical-harmonic gravitational-wave modes—including (3,3) and (4,4)—across binary black hole populations in third-generation detector networks (Cosmic Explorer and Einstein Telescope) compared against upgraded second-generation detectors.
