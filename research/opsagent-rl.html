01 / QUESTION

What are we trying to understand?

How do supervised and preference-based training methods affect agent reliability, tool correctness, and transfer to unseen scenarios?

02 / METHOD

How was it investigated?

  • Built an operations simulator with 7 allow-listed tools and partial observability.
  • Implementing SFT, rejection sampling, DPO/IPO, and multi-turn GRPO on Qwen2.5 agents.
  • Developing simulator-grounded rewards and evaluations of distribution-shift robustness and catastrophic forgetting.
  • Implemented offline fitted Q-iteration and a reproducible evaluation test suite.
03 / EVIDENCE

What do the results support?

  • Simulator and initial evaluation infrastructure developed.
  • Comparative post-training and out-of-distribution results remain in progress.

Research question

How do supervised and preference-based training methods affect agent reliability, tool correctness, and transfer to unseen scenarios?

Approach

  • Built an operations simulator with 7 allow-listed tools and partial observability.
  • Implementing SFT, rejection sampling, DPO/IPO, and multi-turn GRPO on Qwen2.5 agents.
  • Developing simulator-grounded rewards and evaluations of distribution-shift robustness and catastrophic forgetting.
  • Implemented offline fitted Q-iteration and a reproducible evaluation test suite.

Findings & status

  • Simulator and initial evaluation infrastructure developed.
  • Comparative post-training and out-of-distribution results remain in progress.
