Hi, I’m Preet Baxi
Ph.D. researcher · University of Michigan

I study how complex and intelligent systems fail, and how to make them safer and more reliable.

I work across AI safety and gravitational-wave physics. I investigate prompt-injection risks in AI agents, study the reliability of LLM evaluations, and develop signal-validation methods for LIGO. Across both fields, I rely on careful experiments and statistical evidence—not assumptions.

03 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Beyond the research.

The people, experiences, and little curiosities that shape the work.

I love learning something new, and even more, sharing that sense of discovery.

Hi, I’m Preet! I’m a Ph.D. researcher at the University of Michigan. My journey has taken me from aerospace engineering to gravitational-wave science at LIGO and, more recently, to understanding the behavior of AI systems. What connects those chapters is a love of asking difficult questions and building things that work in the real world.

Outside my own research, I really enjoy teaching, mentoring, and conversations that start with “what if?” I’ve also spent time learning to fly—a wonderful reminder that curiosity, preparation, and good judgment belong together. I’m grateful for the people I’ve met along the way, and I hope this corner of the internet shares a little of that spirit.
